# Affect Rule

Represent affect as a structured computational state, for example:
- valence;
- arousal;
- dominant emotion label(s);
- confidence;
- trigger/context;
- desired response stance.

Affect inference is probabilistic. Preserve uncertainty.

Affect influences response planning, wording, pacing and optional prosody. It should not be a decorative emoji layer added after generation.

Do not diagnose mental-health conditions from ordinary conversation.

Avoid manipulative dependency-building behaviors. Preserve user agency.
